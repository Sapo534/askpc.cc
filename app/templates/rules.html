Rules & Code of Conduct

To keep askpc.cc high quality, we strictly enforce simple rules.

1. Zero Toxicity Policy ("No RTFM")

Every expert was once a beginner. Responding with "RTFM", "Google it", or mockingly pointing out basic mistakes is prohibited. Explain the solution or move on.

Details

Not allowed: mockery, condescension, insults, personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, and "just use Linux/Windows" style derailing of someone's question.

Allowed: disagreeing with a solution, correcting factual errors, and pointing to documentation together with a short explanation or the relevant quote.

Rule of thumb: if you cannot help kindly, do not reply. Skipping a thread costs nothing.

Disagreements are fine, attacks on people are not. If a discussion gets heated, report it instead of escalating.

2. Signal-to-Noise Ratio

Format your code blocks, provide logs, hardware specs, and system information when asking questions on the forum.

Details

A good question includes:

  • A clear title describing the problem, not "help pls".
  • Your OS and version, hardware (CPU, GPU, RAM, relevant devices), and software versions.
  • What you expected to happen and what actually happened.
  • What you already tried.
  • Exact error messages and logs in code blocks, not screenshots of text.

Also: search before posting, keep one problem per thread, do not bump or cross-post the same question, and mark the solution when you find it so others benefit.

Incomplete questions may be closed or moved until the missing information is added.

3. No Self-Promotion / Spam

Commercial advertising, referral links, and SEO spam will lead to instant permanent ban.

Details

Counts as spam: ads, affiliate and referral links, link farming, SEO posts, mass-posted identical content, undisclosed promotion of paid products or services, and accounts created only to promote something.

Allowed: mentioning a tool, project, or product when it directly solves the problem being discussed, as long as you disclose your affiliation if you made it or profit from it. Open-source and non-commercial projects are welcome in relevant threads.

Spam results in an immediate permanent ban without prior warning.

4. Off-topic

On the AskPC website, we only allow discussions about computer-related topics. Anything else is off-limits here. For off-topic conversations, we have our own Matrix Space and Discord server.

Details

On topic: hardware, operating systems, software, networking, programming, self-hosting, troubleshooting, and PC gaming from the technical side (performance, drivers, settings).

Off topic: politics, religion, general chit-chat, memes, and anything unrelated to computers. Off-topic threads are moved or removed.

Looking to just chat? Join the Matrix Space or Discord server linked in Community.

5. Enforcement

Moderators act proportionally to the violation.

Details

Typical steps: content edited or removed, then a warning, then a temporary ban, then a permanent ban. Serious violations (spam, harassment, illegal content) may skip straight to a permanent ban.

If you think a moderation decision was a mistake, contact us and we will review it. To report a post, use the report function or contact the moderators.
