Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

1. Minimal Data Collection

We do not track you across the web. We do not use third-party analytics like Google Analytics. We do not sell any user data.

Details

What we store when you register: your username, your email address (used only for account recovery and essential notices), and a hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.

What we store when you use the site: the content you publish (forum posts, comments, edits) together with timestamps and your username.

What we do not do: no fingerprinting, no tracking pixels, no ad networks, no social media widgets, no data sharing or sales to third parties. Pages load only our own scripts and styles.

Third-party services: our Matrix Space and Discord server are separate services with their own privacy policies. Joining them is optional, and nothing is shared with them automatically.

2. Cookies

We only use essential functional cookies to keep you logged in to the forum. No tracking or advertising cookies are set.

Details

We set a single session cookie when you log in. It is needed for authentication and is removed when you log out or when it expires.

The cookie is not used for analytics or profiling, and it is not readable by third parties. Because it is strictly necessary for the service to work, no cookie banner is required.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser at any time, but you will not be able to stay logged in.

3. Server Logs

Standard web server logs (IP address, user agent) are automatically erased after 7 days and used purely for security and DDoS prevention.

Details

What is logged: IP address, user agent, requested URL, response status, and time of request.

Why: detecting abuse, blocking brute-force login attempts, and mitigating DDoS attacks. Logs are not used for advertising or profiling.

Retention: automatically deleted after 7 days. Logs are not included in backups beyond that period.

4. Your Rights

You control your data: you can see it, fix it, and delete it.

Details

Access and correction: you can review and edit your profile data in Settings.

Deletion: you can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us. Your username and email are removed. Public posts may be kept in anonymized form (shown as "deleted user") so that threads and solutions stay readable for others.

Export: on request, we will send you a copy of the data we hold about you.

We respond to such requests within 30 days.

5. Security and Changes

We protect your data in practice, and we tell you when this policy changes.

Details

All connections use HTTPS. Access to the server and database is limited to the site administrators.

If a data breach affects your personal data, we will notify affected users without undue delay.

Significant changes to this policy will be announced on the site, and the "Last updated" date above will change.

Contact: privacy@askpc.cc
