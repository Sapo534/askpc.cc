Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

askpc.cc is a small independent project run by an individual. Contact: privacy@askpc.cc

1. What We Collect

Only what is needed to run your account and the forum. No tracking, no analytics, no ads, no selling of data.

Details

Account: username, email address, a bcrypt-hashed password (never stored in plain text), optional bio and avatar, karma, and account timestamps. Email verification and password reset tokens are stored hashed. Accounts whose email is never verified may be removed.

GitHub login (optional): if you sign in or link with GitHub, we receive your GitHub username, avatar and email address. We never receive your GitHub password.

Content: your threads, replies, votes and file attachments, with timestamps. Posts, avatars and attachments are public and reachable by direct URL. Remove metadata such as location from images before uploading them.

Matrix (optional): if you join our Matrix Space, we store your Matrix account (user ID, display name, avatar) and the messages you send there on our own server. Rooms are public and not end-to-end encrypted, and their history is visible to anyone, including people who have not joined. Your website account and your Matrix account are separate.

Sponsorship (optional): payments are made in USDT on the Solana network to our wallet. We store the transaction hash and the amount linked to your account. The Solana blockchain publicly records the sender wallet address, the amount and the transaction hash, and we cannot erase these records.

Not collected: no fingerprinting, no tracking pixels, no social widgets, no third-party analytics.

2. Cookies

Only functional cookies. No tracking or advertising cookies.

Details

auth_session: keeps you logged in. HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax. Without "Remember me" it is valid for at most 24 hours and is dropped when you close the browser; with "Remember me" it is valid for 30 days. It is removed when you log out.

session: a signed technical cookie used during GitHub login to protect against forged requests.

theme: remembers your light or dark theme choice for one year. It contains no personal data.

The login cookies are strictly necessary, so no cookie banner is shown. Blocking them means you cannot log in.

3. Who Else Receives Data

Only the services needed to operate the site.

Details

Resend (resend.com, using Amazon SES infrastructure) delivers our verification and password reset emails. It receives your email address and the message content, stores them in the United States and keeps delivery logs for 30 days. Transfers outside the EEA are covered by Standard Contractual Clauses.

Cloudflare proxies traffic to askpc.cc, so it processes requests (including IP addresses) in order to deliver and protect the site.

GitHub receives a request when you choose to log in with it.

External images: if a post embeds an image hosted elsewhere, your browser loads it from that server, which can see your IP address.

Other Matrix servers: Matrix is a federated network. Messages in our public rooms are copied to the servers of other participants, and we cannot delete those copies.

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

4. Server Logs and IP Addresses

We keep technical logs only for security and debugging.

Details

Application logs may contain IP addresses, requested URLs and status codes. They are rotated automatically by size and are not kept long-term. IP addresses are also held in memory for rate limiting and brute-force protection.

Logs are not used for advertising or profiling.

5. Your Rights

You can see, fix and delete your data.

Details

Backups: we keep encrypted backups for up to 6 months. Data you delete from the live site may remain in those backups until they are rotated out; backups are used only to restore the site after a failure.

Access and correction: edit your profile in Settings.

Deletion and export: email privacy@askpc.cc from the address on your account, and we will delete your account data (username, email, bio, avatar) or send you a copy of what we hold. We respond within 30 days.

Public posts may be anonymized or removed so threads stay readable. Blockchain records cannot be deleted.

6. Security and Changes

All connections use HTTPS, and access to the server is limited to the administrator.

Details

If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify affected users without undue delay. Significant changes to this policy are announced on the site, and the date above is updated.

Contact: privacy@askpc.cc
